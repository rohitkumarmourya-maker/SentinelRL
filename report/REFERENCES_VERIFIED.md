# Verified primary references

Reference metadata and supporting claims were checked against primary sources on 26 October 2026. These references describe standard methodology, not SentinelRL performance.

1. L. P. Kaelbling, M. L. Littman and A. R. Cassandra. *Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains*. Artificial Intelligence 101 (1998), 99–134. Author-hosted full text: https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf . Supports the POMDP and posterior-belief framing.
2. V. Mnih et al. *Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning*. Nature 518 (2015), 529–533. Publisher: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236 . Supports DQN replay and target-network background.
3. H. van Hasselt, A. Guez and D. Silver. *Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning*. AAAI 2016. Author-submitted record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461 . Supports separating action selection from target evaluation.
4. Z. Wang, T. Schaul, M. Hessel, H. van Hasselt, M. Lanctot and N. de Freitas. *Dueling Network Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning*. ICML, PMLR 48 (2016), 1995–2003. Publisher: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/wangf16.html . Confirms separate state-value and action-advantage estimators.
