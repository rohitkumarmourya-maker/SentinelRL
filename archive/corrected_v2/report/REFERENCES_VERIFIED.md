# Reference verification

These sources support standard RL concepts and algorithms only. They are not evidence of SentinelRL performance or source-code provenance.

Verified online on 26 October 2026 against primary sources:

1. Leslie Pack Kaelbling, Michael L. Littman, Anthony R. Cassandra. *Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains*. Artificial Intelligence 101 (1998), 99–134. Author-hosted full text confirms metadata and POMDP treatment: https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf
2. Volodymyr Mnih et al. *Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning*. Nature 518 (2015), 529–533. Publisher page confirms title, authors, volume, pages, date, DQN: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236
3. Hado van Hasselt, Arthur Guez, David Silver. *Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning*. AAAI 2016; author-submitted preprint arXiv:1509.06461. The arXiv record confirms title/authors, AAAI 2016 note, and the Double DQN mechanism: https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461

No external source code has been fetched or incorporated into the report. The topology is a design illustration; the learning and comparison figures use completed raw experiment logs.
